# EconSim hosted financial literacy workspace
Audience: Nigerian and African students and emerging strategists.
Concept: a financial intelligence terminal where research leads to decisions.
Experience: responsive utility-first dashboard with light, purposeful transitions.
Animation mode: non-animated — chosen based on the user's request for a sharp, interactive dashboard rather than a cinematic landing page.
Palette: slate navy #142841, jade #247965, soft grey #f5f7fa.
Typography: Space Grotesk headings, DM Sans body.
Screens: onboarding preview, dashboard, predictor, market intelligence, boardroom lobby, waiting room, live simulation, feedback, wallet, portfolio, leaderboard, profile, settings.
Safety: virtual currency only, mock market probabilities; scenario hidden until demonstration begins.
Architecture: single-page React interaction on TanStack Start SSR route. Browser-local demo persistence, no claim of real authentication or AI adjudication.
Publicity: deploy as an unlisted standalone website, not to a community feed.
